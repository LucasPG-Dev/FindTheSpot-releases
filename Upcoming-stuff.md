## Upcoming features!
- Single map lobbies.(Rounds locked at how many spots that map has)
- Start together.(On by default)At match start, everyone is brought to the hosts position.
- Warp mode. Optional travel setting: Everyone is warped to the rounds map, so it's about finding the spot instead of the map
  - Rounds are shorter
  - REP is halved
- Player maps. each players current map shows on the website and in-game lobby screen(not during rounds ofc)

### During a round
- Anonymous guesses. Only shows who guessed what at the end of the round.
- Guessed players go invis. (Only works with ACN) Players who have guessed aren't visible till the round ends.
- New cut timer. If a lobby has 4+ ppl, the timer is cut at the second guess instead of first(cut is 45/30/25s by difficulty).
- Timer fix. Clock matches the one in website.
- Start countdown. Now only plays before the first round(every round in warp mode).
- Full boost when you load into a map mid game.

### Comp mode (Subject to change/under construction)
- When someone finds all spots, the timer is cut to 60s left for everyone else.
- Comp games now end when:
  - Everyone has found all spots
  - Timer runs out
  - In lobbies of 3+, only one player is left
- If you finish early, your time is saved.

### After a match and in the lobby
- Where is everyone. Players on your map get a pillar and a board lists everyone else's map.
- Go to player. A button in the in-game lobby screen sends you to another player, they see a "X is coming to you!" message.
