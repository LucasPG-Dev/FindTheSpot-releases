# Find the Spot

**Find the Spot is basically a BRC GeoGuessr.** Get a photo of a random spot, figure out where it was taken, head over there and lock in your guess!
The closer you are, the more points you get.

## What's in it?

### Solo mode
- **Classic:** One pic at a time, your guess is where you stand.
- **Comp:** A bunch of pics at once, found automatically when you get close!
- **Easy / Normal / Hard** difficulties, plus a **Challenge** mode where the pics start blurred, pixelated or scrambled (if you wanna suffer a bit).
- Personal bests for every mode.

### Multiplayer
- Make or join lobbies through the [website](https://brcguessr.tailf9b85f.ts.net) and play Classic or Comp together!
- With All City Network(ACN) you get linked automatically, without it you just enter a short code.
- Lobbies with 2+ players give **REP** if you have the AMT Casino mod.

### Custom maps
- Works with MapStation maps! Lobbies only use custom maps everyone has installed.
- Want spots for a map that doesn't have any yet? Drop the name in the suggestion box on the website.

> Currently only Void Valley is in the custom map pool. (v1.0.0)

## How to play
1. Open the **Find the Spot** app on your phone and press **Play**.
2. You get 5 seconds to study the pic before the clock starts.
3. Figure out which map it's on, get there, and stand where the photo was taken.
4. Press **Lock in guess** in the app (or use the quick menu). One guess per round!

Press **F8** in-game any time for the full How to play.

## Controls
| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **M** | Quick menu: travel straight to any map, or guess |
| **G** | In the quick menu: lock in your guess / start the next round |
| **F6** | Photo size: small, large or hidden |
| **F8** | How to play |
| **,** / **.** | Comp mode: previous / next photo |

All keys can be changed in the config (`com.chimp.brcguessr.cfg`).

## Multiplayer
1. In the app, choose **Multiplayer**, then **Open lobby screen**.
2. The website opens in your browser. With All City Network you're signed in already; without it, type the 4-letter code from your phone.
3. Make a lobby or join one. The host picks the mode, difficulty,  maps and starts the match.
4. Every round shows up in-game by itself. Once someone guesses, everyone else has 30 seconds left!

## Optional mods
None of these are required, they just add stuff:
- **MapStation:** custom map spots.
- **All City Network:** multiplayer links with your ACN name to the website without need of a code.
- **AMT Casino:** collect REP from multiplayer matches.

> **Tip: play with props on, some pics have props in them that help you find the spot!**
